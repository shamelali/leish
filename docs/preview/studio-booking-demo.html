Leish! Studio Booking Engine — interactive demo (no database; state kept in your browser)
How “any studio” works

Configure the studio → watch availability compute live

This is a browser-side re-implementation of the booking engine shipped in the repo (get_available_slots() · book_appointment() · booking_transition()). Everything you change on the left (weekly hours, rooms/artists, deposit, buffers, advance notice) is instantly reflected in the customer flow on the right — exactly like the real engine reads studio_settings. Pay, then try the double-booking race to see the engine’s conflict guard.
